YouTube RPM by Niche in 2026: Every Category Compared

By PlayMETRIC Team  •  Published July 21, 2026  •  Updated October 23, 2026

YouTube does not pay a fixed amount per view. RPM depends on the channel, audience, format, period and revenue sources. Numbers below are illustrative assumptions, not verified 2026 earnings data.

TL;DR
  • CPM measures advertiser cost per 1,000 ad impressions. RPM measures creator revenue after revenue sharing per 1,000 video views, including unmonetized views and revenue from ads, Premium, memberships and other eligible features. Shorts RPM uses engaged views. Revenue-share rules differ by monetization feature.

Why advertiser demand can vary by audience

Advertiser demand can vary with audience location, time of year and available ad formats. A niche label alone does not determine a channel's RPM.

Compare relevant revenue reports in your own YouTube Analytics before changing your content strategy. A hypothetical niche multiplier is not evidence that a topic change will increase earnings.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between RPM and CPM?
CPM measures advertiser cost per 1,000 ad impressions. RPM measures creator revenue after revenue sharing per 1,000 video views, including unmonetized views and revenue from ads, Premium, memberships and other eligible features. Shorts RPM uses engaged views. Revenue-share rules differ by monetization feature.
What is a good RPM in 2026?
There is no universal good RPM threshold. Compare your own channel over comparable periods and inspect revenue sources, audience mix and format. The scenario table is not an industry benchmark.
Which YouTube niche has the highest RPM?
We do not have a verified dataset establishing a highest-paying niche. The table orders illustrative assumptions, not measured market rankings.
Can I increase my RPM without changing niches?
Yes: make more videos on money-adjacent topics within your niche, grow your US/UK/Canada audience share, make videos over 8 minutes to enable mid-roll ads, and publish more in Q4 when advertiser spending peaks.
Do Shorts use the same niche RPM table?
No. Shorts use a separate revenue-sharing model and RPM based on engaged views. There is no universal payout per 1,000 views or fixed ratio to long-form earnings. Use your own YouTube Analytics data; calculator amounts are illustrative assumptions.
