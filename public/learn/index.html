Frequently asked questions

Short answers about revenue concepts, illustrative calculator assumptions, and thumbnail specifications.

What is the difference between RPM and CPM?
CPM measures advertiser cost per 1,000 ad impressions. RPM measures creator revenue after revenue sharing per 1,000 video views, including unmonetized views and revenue from ads, Premium, memberships and other eligible features. Shorts RPM uses engaged views. Revenue-share rules differ by monetization feature.
How much do YouTube Shorts pay in 2026?
Shorts use a separate revenue-sharing model and RPM based on engaged views. There is no universal payout per 1,000 views or fixed ratio to long-form earnings. Use your own YouTube Analytics data; calculator amounts are illustrative assumptions.
What size should a YouTube thumbnail be?
Upload 1280 x 720 pixels at 16:9, under 2MB, as JPG, PNG, static GIF, or WEBP. That is YouTube's recommended size and the HD file stored as maxresdefault.
Is it legal to download a YouTube thumbnail?
A public thumbnail is not automatically free to reuse. Copyright exceptions depend on the facts and jurisdiction; in the US, courts assess fair use case by case. Use your own work, obtain permission, or establish a valid legal basis before reusing another creator's image.
